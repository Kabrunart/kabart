Eugene Kabrun

The World In 3D

Born in Kyiv, Ukraine, Eugene Kabrun has pursued his passion for fine art since childhood. Over the years, his artistic journey has expanded across genres and mediums, ranging from landscapes and still lifes to portraits. Through his diverse painting styles, Eugene seeks to capture moments of beauty and emotion, creating works that resonate deeply with viewers.

Now based in Manitoba, Canada, Eugene has been recognized by both local and national media outlets, including CityNews Winnipeg, Trucknews, and The Carillon. His paintings have been exhibited in galleries across Canada and internationally, reaching audiences in a variety of settings and communities.
